How to Play

It's a foggy night and you've taken shelter in a roadside tea house. Your host is kind. She is also not human. Find the proof before the candle burns down, then choose the door that leads home.

The candle

The candle has six marks. Examining something burns one. When the last mark burns, it's midnight.

Talking to her

Once you've noticed something odd, you can bring it up with your host. That's free — she'll explain it away. Then you can let it go, or press her. Pressing is how you prove things, but every press makes her more wary of you — read how she's described under the candle. Push too far and she stops pretending.

Clues

Noticing something strange isn't proof. A clue is confirmed only once you've seen it and pressed her about it. Confirmed clues are listed next to the scene with a ✓.

The doors

At the last mark there are two doors. Only one leads back to the road. The more clues you've confirmed, the easier they are to tell apart.

Controls

  • Touch or mouse: tap a choice.
  • Keyboard: Tab and Shift+Tab move between choices; Enter or Space picks one.

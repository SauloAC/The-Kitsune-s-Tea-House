Privacy

Updated 8 October 2026.

This game has no account, no server of its own and collects nothing about you. No ads, no analytics, no cookies. This page says exactly what the three things kept on your device are, and which two companies see your request simply because they deliver the files.

What stays on your device

Three things, kept by your own browser, which never leave the device and which nobody — including me — can read:

  • the language you chose;
  • whether the music is on, and at what volume;
  • which loop was playing and how far in, until you close the tab.

There is no identifier, no profile and no record of the choices you make in the game. A night in progress lives only in the page's memory: reloading starts it over.

What does not exist

No account, no sign-up, no email. No advertising. No analytics or audience measurement. No cookies. No trackers. Nothing is shared with anyone, because there is nothing to share.

The two typefaces

The pages load Shippori Mincho and Zen Kaku Gothic New from Google Fonts. To deliver them, Google receives your IP address and the usual technical details of the request, as any server does when it hands over a file. It is the only third-party request in the whole game. Serving the fonts from this site instead would remove it; if that is done, this page will say so.

Where the game is hosted

The site is served by GitHub Pages. GitHub logs requests, including the IP address, to run and protect the service, as set out in GitHub's own privacy statement.

The copy kept for playing offline

Once you have opened the game, your browser keeps a copy of its own files — the pages, the pictures, the music, the two typefaces — so it works with no network and can be installed like an app. The copy holds the game, not you: there is nothing in it about what you did. After that first visit the typefaces come from the copy, so the request to Google stops happening.

How to erase what is kept

Clearing this site's data in your browser erases all three, and the offline copy of the game with them. No copy exists anywhere else, so there is nothing else to ask for and nobody to ask.

Children

The game collects no data from anyone, of any age.
